§ 01 — About

A researcher
curious about the cosmos.

I am a physicist and researcher based in Kolhapur, India, currently pursuing research in theoretical astrophysics and space-based instrumentation. My work spans the analysis of pulsar candidates, the orbital mechanics of black holes with dark matter halos, and the development of real-time GNSS ionospheric forecasting models.

Beyond theory, I lead the Great Lunar Expedition at the Colorado Space Grant Consortium — a team deploying a LunaSat to the Lunar South Pole to study the Moon's magnetic, thermal, and seismic properties. I have also conducted research at the Green Bank Observatory (USA) and the Instituto de Astrofísica e Ciências do Espaço (Portugal).

I hold an M.Sc. in Physics from the Institute of Science, Mumbai (CGPA 9.13), and currently serve as a Lecturer at Rajaram College, where I teach and mentor the next generation of physicists.
